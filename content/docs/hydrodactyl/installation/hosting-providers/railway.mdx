This guide walks you through deploying Hydrodactyl on [Railway](https://railway.com/) using the community-maintained one-click template. The template provisions the panel together with MariaDB and Redis.

<Callout type="warn">
  This template is **unofficial and community-maintained**. It is not built or
  supported by the Hydrodactyl team, and issues with the template itself should
  be raised with its author rather than the Hydrodactyl maintainers.
</Callout>

<Callout type="info">
  Need help with Hydrodactyl itself? Join our [Hydrodactyl
  Discord](https://discord.gg/sK686yHdaK) or open a discussion on
  [GitHub](https://github.com/blueprintframework/hydrodactyl/discussions).
</Callout>

## Prerequisites

- A [Railway](https://railway.com/) account
- A domain name (recommended) — Railway can also issue you a `*.up.railway.app` domain
- A separate machine or provider for [Pterodactyl Wings](https://pterodactyl.io/wings/1.0/installing.html) if you want to run game servers. Railway only hosts the panel, its database, and its cache.

## What the template creates

Deploying the [Hydrodactyl template](https://railway.com/deploy/hydrodactyl) creates three services in a new Railway project:

| Service    | Image                                     | Purpose                        |
| ---------- | ----------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Hydrodactyl | `ghcr.io/blueprintframework/hydrodactyl` | The panel (Nginx + PHP)        |
| MariaDB    | `mariadb:12`                              | Panel database                 |
| Redis      | `redis:8.2`                               | Cache, sessions, and queues    |

Railway automatically wires the panel to MariaDB and Redis over its private network and generates the required secrets (`APP_KEY`, `HASHIDS_SALT`, database and Redis passwords) for you.

## Panel installation

1. **Deploy the template**

   Open the [Hydrodactyl template](https://railway.com/deploy/hydrodactyl), sign in to Railway if prompted, and click **Deploy Now**. Choose the workspace and project you want it in, then confirm.

2. **Wait for the services to come up**

   Railway builds all three services. The panel reports healthy once its `/up` endpoint responds and the database migrations have run. You can follow progress in the **Deployments** tab of the Hydrodactyl service.

3. **Expose the panel**

   Open the Hydrodactyl service, go to **Settings → Networking**, and click **Generate Domain**. To use your own domain instead, add it under **Custom Domain** and follow Railway's DNS instructions.

4. **Point `APP_URL` at your domain**

   The template derives `APP_URL` from the Railway-provided domain. If you attached a custom domain, update the `APP_URL` variable on the Hydrodactyl service to `https://your-domain` and redeploy. The panel uses this value for links and for the queue/cron jobs.

5. **Create your administrator account**

   Visit the panel at the configured URL. With no accounts on the system yet, the panel's built-in initial admin creation suite will guide you through creating the first administrator account.

<Callout type="note">
  Railway terminates TLS at its edge, so leave `LE_EMAIL` unset on the
  Hydrodactyl service — HTTPS is handled for you.
</Callout>

## Post-installation configuration

1. **Panel setup**:
   - Access your panel at the configured domain
   - Log in with your admin account
   - Navigate to **Admin → Locations** and create a new location
   - Navigate to **Nodes** and add your Wings instance

2. **Email configuration** (Admin → Settings):
   - Set `MAIL_*` variables on the Hydrodactyl service and redeploy
   - Recommended providers:
     - Resend
     - Mailgun
     - AWS SES

## Maintenance

1. **Updating Hydrodactyl**:
   - The template is pinned to a specific image tag. To update, change the image
     on the Hydrodactyl service (e.g. `ghcr.io/blueprintframework/hydrodactyl:v6.4.1`)
     and redeploy.

## Troubleshooting

Common issues and solutions:

- **Build or deploy failures**:
  - Check the deployment logs for the failing service
  - Verify the MariaDB and Redis services are running before the panel
- **Panel loads but assets or links use the wrong URL**:
  - Make sure `APP_URL` matches the public domain exactly, including `https://`
- **"Waiting for database connection" in the logs**:
  - MariaDB is still starting up; the panel retries automatically
